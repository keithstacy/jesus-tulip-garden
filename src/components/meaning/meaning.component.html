Life's Meaning

Why are we here, anyway?

The Westminster Shorter Catechism states:

What is the chief end of man?
Man's chief end is to glorify God, and to enjoy Him forever.

The Bible says:

In Your presence is fullness of joy;
At Your right hand are pleasures forevermore.
—Psalm 16:11 (NKJV)

And my soul shall be joyful in the Lord;
It shall rejoice in His salvation.
—Psalm 35:9

My soul shall be satisfied as with marrow and fatness,
And my mouth shall praise You with joyful lips.
—Psalm 63:5

These things I have spoken to you, that My joy may remain in you, and that your joy may be full.
—John 15:11

Im­a­gine that! We can spend for­ever and ever in a glor­ious cy­cle of love in which we make God a­maz­ing­ly happy, and God makes us a­maz­ing­ly hap­py in return.

And that for­ev­er can be­gin now.

Your life can have a meaning exalted above the grinding of this world. Your life can be changed in ways you can't begin to imagine. Your life can have a purpose with results that will shine for all of eternity.
