Who We Are
Our Mission
Our mission is simple: to glorify God and to share the good news of His grace, saving sinners like us, through Jesus Christ. As the Bible says in Acts 4:12, "And there is salvation in no one else, for there is no other name under heaven given among men by which we must be saved."
Our Vision
Our vision is multifaceted. We pray that:
  • Each one of us has a life-giving faith encounter with Jesus Christ. He offers the gift of saving faith to all those who call on Him.
    • John 3:1-18; Ephesians 2:1-10; Joel 2:32; Acts 2:21; Romans 10:13
  • Each one of us makes God's word - The Bible - the focus of our living and decision-making.
    • Psalm 19:7-11; 2 Timothy 3:16
  • We effectively reach into our community as individuals and as a church.
    • Matthew 5:13-16; 1 Peter 2:9-12
Our Values
Our core values—what we call the five Solas—are simply five points that detail our Reformed teaching on things like:
  • The Bible
  • Faith
  • Grace
  • Jesus
  • Life's meaning
These anc­ient teach­ings find their roots in the Gos­pel mes­sage. They were re­dis­cover­ed during the medi­eval Prot­est­ant Ref­orm­a­tion, and cont­inue to be more than just his­tory—they are liv­ing truths to be re­ceived and em­braced.
They re­mind us of what is cent­ral to Scrip­ture and what is crit­ical­ly im­port­ant for faith­ful, Bible-based church­es to teach, preach, and live out before the watch­ing world.
