Who We Are
Our Mission
Our mis­sion is sim­ple: to glor­ify God and to share the good news of His grace, sav­ing sin­ners like us, through Jesus Christ. As the Bible says in Acts 4:12, "And there is salv­ation in no one else, for there is no other name under hea­ven giv­en a­mong men by which we must be saved."
Our Vision
Our vision is multifaceted. We pray that:
  • Each one of us has a life-giv­ing faith en­count­er with Jesus Christ. He of­fers the gift of sav­ing faith to all those who call on Him.
    • John 3:1-18; Ephe­sians 2:1-10; Joel 2:32; Acts 2:21; Rom­ans 10:13
  • Each one of us makes God's word - The Bible - the focus of our liv­ing and de­cis­ion-mak­ing.
    • Psalm 19:7-11; 2 Tim­othy 3:16
  • We ef­fect­ive­ly reach into our com­mun­ity as in­div­id­u­als and as a church.
    • Matthew 5:13-16; 1 Peter 2:9-12
Our Values
Our core val­ues—what we call the five Solas—are sim­ply five points that de­tail our Re­formed teach­ing on things like:
  • The Bible
  • Faith
  • Grace
  • Jesus
  • Life's meaning
These anc­ient teach­ings find their roots in the Gos­pel mes­sage. They were re­dis­cover­ed dur­ing the medi­eval Prot­est­ant Ref­orm­a­tion, and cont­inue to be more than just his­tory—they are liv­ing truths to be re­ceived and em­braced.

They re­mind us of what is cent­ral to Scrip­ture and what is crit­ical­ly im­port­ant for faith­ful, Bible-based church­es to teach, preach, and live out be­fore the watch­ing world.
