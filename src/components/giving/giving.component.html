What We Believe About
Giving

We teach that when some­one joins our church fam­ily, they should pray care­ful­ly about what they should reg­u­lar­ly give to sup­port the work of God here at St. Paul's. But we don't ask our vi­si­tors to con­trib­ute un­less they want to. And for those who do be­long to our fam­ily, we trust them to do what God calls them to do—we don't check up on them, and we only track what mem­bers give so they can claim the don­a­tions for tax pur­pos­es. But we do seek to share what the Bible teach­es about giv­ing, be­cause it is an im­port­ant sub­ject that God talks about a lot in the Scrip­ture.

What the Bible says about giving

In the Old Testament God com­mand­ed that a tithe, ten per­cent, of all that He has prov­id­ent­ial­ly blessed us with be giv­en back to God. We read in Leviticus 27:32, "con­cern­ing the tithe of the herd or the flock, of what­ever pas­ses un­der the rod, the tenth one shall be holy to the LORD," (see also Numbers 18:26, Deuteronomy 14:24, 2 Chronicles 31:5). Even with the idea of of­fer­ing the first fruits (Leviticus 2:12) giv­ing must be done trust­ing God will pro­vide (Proverbs 3:9-10). The tithe was for the sup­port of the Tab­ern­acle, and la­ter, the Tem­ple and the work of min­is­try. It was also for the up­keep and re­pair of the tem­ple (Nehemiah 10:38, Haggai 1:4-11). It was also used to meet the needs of for­eign­ers, orph­ans and wid­ows (Deuteronomy 26:12-13).

Now since the New Test­am­ent builds on and clar­if­ies the Old Test­am­ent, what does our un­change­able God (Malachi 3:6) de­sire of us now as His church? In the NT we still see the com­mand to sup­port the min­is­try of the word of God (1 Timothy 5:18), widows (1 Timothy 5:9-10) and the poor (Romans 15:26). While Christ ful­fills the Law of Moses, we still are to obey the moral law out of thank­ful­ness. No­where do we see a com­mand that tith­ing is done. Christ even prais­es the wid­ow who gave her two mites (Luke 21:3-4). Why? Because of her heart at­ti­tude! Now that Christ, not with sil­ver or gold but His pre­cious blood has re­deemed us, we must now look at giv­ing back to the Lord with a new att­it­ude! "Not grudgingly or of necessity; for God loves a cheerful giver" (2 Corinthians 9:7). Int­er­est­ing­ly enough, if God puts the burd­en on a heart, be­cause of abil­ity (1 Corinthians 16:2, 1 Peter 4:10), the New Test­am­ent even ex­pands the Old Test­am­ent com­mand tell­ing us to give in lib­er­al­ity (Romans 12:8) know­ing "it is more blessed to give than re­ceive" (Acts 20:35). Our pur­pose now, with e­ter­nal life in view, is to be rich to God stor­ing up treas­ures for the life to come (1 Timothy 6:17-19).
